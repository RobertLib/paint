How it works

  1. Drop your image — Rectangle select is ready.
  2. Drag over the face, plate or text you want to hide.
  3. Click Blur or Pixelate in the task bar, then Export.

Blur or pixelate?

Both hide the content, and the strength grows with the size of the selection, so even a large face is fully unrecognizable after one click. Blur gives a soft, smooth patch that blends into a photo — good for faces in the background or a street scene. Pixelate turns the area into large blocks; it looks deliberate, which is what you want when redacting a screenshot or a document. For the most sensitive details, such as passwords or account numbers, you can also cover the area with a solid Rectangle, which leaves nothing of the original.

Selecting exactly what to hide

Rectangle select is ideal for text, plates and screenshots. Use Ellipse select for faces, the Lasso for irregular shapes, or the Magic wand to pick an area of similar color. Hold Shift while selecting to add more areas, so you can blur several faces in one go. Feather softens the selection edge, so a blurred patch fades into the photo instead of ending in a hard line. Blur and Pixelate always need a selection; with nothing selected, the editor asks you to select what to hide first.

Blurring a background and other effects

Blur works the other way round too: select your subject with the Lasso, choose Invert selection in the Edit menu and apply Blur to soften everything except the person, for a simple portrait look. For gentle, hand-controlled softening, paint with the Blur brush exactly where you want it, for example over a distracting sign. The Filters panel also offers Soft blur and Pixelate, which apply to the whole image or to the current selection.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone un-blur the hidden area?

Not from the exported file. Blurring and pixelating replace the original pixels, and the strength scales with the selection so that the content is fully hidden. For passwords or ID numbers, a solid black Rectangle over the area is the most certain option.

Is it safe to blur sensitive documents here?

Yes. The image is opened and blurred inside your browser and is never uploaded, so the unredacted version doesn’t pass through anyone else’s server. Just remember to share the exported file, not the original.

How do I blur several faces at once?

Hold Shift while drawing additional selections to add them to the first one. When every face is selected, click Blur or Pixelate once and all of the selected areas are hidden together.

How do I blur part of a screenshot?

Paste the screenshot straight into the page with Ctrl/⌘+V, or drop the saved file. Select names, emails or numbers with Rectangle select, click Pixelate and export as PNG, which keeps the rest of the text sharp.
