How it works

  1. Drop the .webp file — the Export dialog opens with JPEG selected.
  2. Adjust the quality, or switch to PNG to keep transparency.
  3. Click Download to save the converted image.

Why WebP files cause trouble

Many websites serve images as WebP because the files are small and load quickly. When you save one of those images, you get a .webp file — and older photo viewers, office programs, print shops and some upload forms still don’t accept it. Converting to JPG gives you a file that opens practically everywhere. What you see in the picture stays the same; only the way it is stored changes.

JPG or PNG?

Choose JPEG for photographs and for anything you want to print, share by email or attach to a document. Choose PNG when the WebP has a transparent background, such as a logo or a sticker: JPEG would fill those areas with white, while PNG keeps them see-through. PNG is also the better choice for screenshots and illustrations with text. Not sure whether your image has transparency? Look at the canvas in the editor — a checkerboard pattern marks transparent areas.

Fix the image before you save

Because the WebP is open in a full editor, you can improve it before converting. Crop away a website banner or border, resize it for a document, or adjust brightness and contrast in the Adjust panel. Then click the Export button to open the dialog again. Quality 85–90 is a safe choice for JPG, and the estimate shows the resulting file size before you download.

Frequently asked questions

Will I lose quality converting WebP to JPG?

A little, since both are usually lossy formats and the image is compressed again. At quality 85–90 the difference is very hard to see. Avoid converting the same picture back and forth repeatedly, as every lossy save adds small artifacts.

Why won’t my computer open WebP files?

WebP is newer than JPEG and PNG, and support arrived in different programs at different times. Up-to-date browsers open it without problems, but older image viewers, office suites and some online forms don’t. Converting to JPG or PNG is the simplest fix.

Can I convert JPG to WebP too?

Yes. Open any JPG or PNG and choose WebP in the Export dialog. WebP files are usually smaller than JPEGs at similar quality and can keep transparency, which makes them a good choice for websites.
