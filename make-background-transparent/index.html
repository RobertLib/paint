How it works

  1. Drop your image — the Magic wand is ready.
  2. Click the background, Shift-click to add more, and press Delete.
  3. Refine the edges if needed, then Export as PNG or WebP.

When this works best

The Magic wand selects pixels by color, so it shines on plain, evenly colored backgrounds: logos on white, product shots on a studio backdrop, scanned signatures, icons and clip art. A busy background — a street, a forest, a patterned sofa — has too many different colors for one click and needs more manual work with the Lasso and Eraser. There is no AI subject detection, so you stay in control of exactly which pixels disappear. A checkerboard pattern shows the areas that are already transparent.

Getting a clean selection

Tolerance decides how similar a color must be to get selected: low values for crisp graphics, higher values for backgrounds with shadows or JPEG noise. Shift-click adds areas, such as the gaps inside letters like “o” or “a”, and Alt-click (Option-click on a Mac) removes areas you grabbed by accident. Turning off Contiguous selects the color everywhere in the image at once. After deleting, zoom in on the edges: a pale fringe left behind usually means the tolerance was too low.

Refining edges and saving

For non-destructive work, use a layer mask instead of deleting: after selecting the background, invert the selection and create a mask from it. The background is hidden rather than erased, and painting black or white on the mask hides or reveals pixels again, so a stray click is easy to fix. The Eraser cleans up anything the wand missed. Trim transparent edges in the Image menu cuts the empty margin around a logo. Export as PNG for compatibility or WebP for a smaller file; JPEG can’t store transparency and fills it with white.

Frequently asked questions

Can it remove the background from a portrait automatically?

No. Paint Studio has no AI background removal. You can still cut out a person with the Lasso, the Magic wand and a layer mask, but hair and complex backgrounds take patience. Plain backgrounds, like a white wall, are much quicker.

Why does my transparent image have a white background after saving?

It was most likely saved as JPEG, which has no transparency and fills those areas with white. Export again and choose PNG or WebP. Also check your viewer: some apps display transparent areas as white or black even when the file is transparent.

How do I make a signature transparent?

Photograph or scan the signature on white paper, then raise Contrast in the Adjust panel so the paper becomes whiter and the ink darker. Click the paper with the Magic wand, delete it, use Trim transparent edges and export as PNG.

How do I remove a white background from a logo?

Click the white area with the Magic wand, then Shift-click inside closed letters — or turn off Contiguous to grab all the white at once, as long as the logo itself contains no white. Press Delete and export as PNG.
